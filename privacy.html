Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

ClipChaos is a private tool used only by the owner of the Clip Chaos accounts to publish short highlight clips to their own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What we access: when the account owner connects TikTok, we receive an access token and basic profile information (display name) for that one account, and we read view, like, comment and share counts of videos we published.

How we use it: only to upload videos the owner has prepared and to show the owner how those videos perform. We do not access other users' data, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion: tokens are stored in a protected file on a private server accessible only to the owner. Disconnecting the app in TikTok settings, or emailing us, deletes the stored token immediately.
