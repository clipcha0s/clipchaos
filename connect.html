Connect your TikTok account

ClipChaos publishes your finished highlight clips to your own TikTok account and shows how they perform.

What ClipChaos will be able to do:
  • user.info.basic – read your display name to show which account is connected
  • video.publish – post the clips you approve directly to your TikTok profile
  • video.list – read views, likes, comments and shares of those clips for your dashboard
